Exercise 2

Measured with bash ex2.sh on Ubuntu 22.04 under WSL. The pager used Aging
and the same sequence of 15 references in each case.

Pages   Frames   TLB entries   TLB misses   Page hits   Disk accesses
5       2        1             13/15        4/15        13
10      2        2             11/15        4/15        13
10      3        2             11/15        7/15        10

With two frames, doubling the TLB size lowered the miss ratio from 86.67%
to 73.33% while the page hit rate stayed at 26.67%. At ten virtual pages,
adding a frame raised the page hit rate to 46.67%. The TLB miss ratio
remained 73.33% on this trace. A TLB miss can still find the page in RAM.
