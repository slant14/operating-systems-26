Lab 07 Sergei Anisimov CSE-04
Exercise 1 — Memory allocator

Environment: Ubuntu 22.04 under WSL, GCC 11.4.0, -O2.
Input: queries.txt with 54 queries (34 allocate, 20 clear).
Each run resets the array of 10,000,000 unsigned integers for each algorithm.
Reported times are the mean of five CPU-time measurements from clock().
Throughput is 54 divided by the mean CPU time.

Algorithm      CPU seconds     queries/s
First Fit         0.120048        449.82
Best Fit          0.333401        161.97
Worst Fit         0.515309        104.79

First Fit had the highest throughput on this trace.
First Fit stops as soon as it finds enough contiguous cells.
Best Fit checks free runs for the smallest adequate block.
Worst Fit selects the largest free run. Both scan the full array.
These timings describe this query trace and do not measure fragmentation.
