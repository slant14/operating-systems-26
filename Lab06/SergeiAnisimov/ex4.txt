Exercise 4 — Round Robin

The supplied trace with a quantum of two ticks gave average turnaround
of 13.43 ticks and average waiting time of 9.14 ticks. FCFS gave 12.29
and 8.00. SJF gave 9.14 and 4.86.

The dispatch order was 4, 3, 5, 4, 0, 3, 1, 6, 3, 1, 6, 3, 2, 1, 6, 2.
A worker that uses its whole quantum is suspended and re-enters the
queue. Jobs that arrive during a slice join the queue before the
preempted worker. This gives jobs earlier opportunities to run even
though average completion metrics were higher on this trace.
