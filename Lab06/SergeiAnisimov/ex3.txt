Exercise 3 — Non-preemptive SJF

The supplied trace dispatched jobs in the order 4, 5, 0, 1, 6, 2, 3.
Average turnaround was 9.14 ticks and average waiting time was 4.86
ticks. FCFS on the same input gave 12.29 and 8.00 ticks.

After job 4 finishes, SJF selects the shortest arrived job, which is
job 5. This lowers the average waiting time on this trace. Job 3 waits
until the end because its eight-tick burst is longer than the other
available bursts. SJF does not interrupt a running job.
