Exercise 2 — FCFS

On Ubuntu 22.04 under WSL, the supplied trace dispatched jobs in the
order 4, 3, 5, 0, 1, 6, 2. Average turnaround was 12.29 ticks and
average waiting time was 8.00 ticks.

With TRI_BASE=100, the worker for job 4 started after 15 instead of
165573 in the sampled run. The worker starts its search at a lower
random value. The dispatch order and scheduling metrics stayed the same
because they depend on arrival and burst times.

With all arrival times set to zero, the tie-break by index gave order
0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6. Average turnaround was 16.71 ticks and average
waiting time was 12.43 ticks.

The scripts default to one second per tick. The comparison runs used
LAB06_TICK_US=50000 and checked each row against a separate simulation.
