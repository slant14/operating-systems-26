./pager random 4 2 > pager_output.txt 2>&1 &
pid=$!
sleep 1
./mmu 4 R0 R1 W1 R0 R2 W2 R0 R3 W2 "$pid" > mmu_output.txt 2>&1
wait "$pid"

review:
all three algorithms were tested with the same data. random had the best
hit ratio in this test (0.5556) and the fewest disk accesses (5).
nfu and aging had the same hit ratio (0.4444), but aging
had one more disk access because it replaced a dirty page.
these results depend on the request sequence, so random is not always
better than nfu or aging.
